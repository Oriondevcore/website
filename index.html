How Ghost Routing Works

Your guests chat with James. James understands what they need. Instead of bouncing between WhatsApp, SMS, and email, James routes the request directly to the right staff member who is actually on-site right now.

The Technology Behind It

When a guest requests housekeeping, our system scans your hotel network to find which housekeeping staff members are currently on-site (we identify them by their phone's unique network address, called a MAC address). We don't use GPS or tracking devices.

The ticket goes directly to the staff member who is nearest or most available, not to a general queue. This means faster service, no lost messages, and no staff member wondering if they have a new task.

All of this happens on your own hotel network. No data leaves your property except for billing and analytics (which we anonymize and keep for 2 years per POPIA regulations).

Documentation & Compliance

POPIA Compliance

Data protection & privacy

Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA)

All guest data is handled with strict compliance to South Africa's POPIA regulations:

  • Personal data (name, email, room number) is automatically deleted 48 hours after guest checkout
  • Anonymized insights (age range, preferences, complaint patterns) are retained for 2 years to help optimize your operations
  • Guests can request 100% data deletion at any time
  • All data is encrypted in transit and at rest
  • Biometric data (if used) is processed only during the guest's stay and not retained

Your data never leaves your property except for billing and analytics.

Ghost Routing Technology

How local network routing works

Ghost Routing: The Local Network Intelligence System

Orion uses your hotel's Wi-Fi network to route guest requests to the right staff member, without GPS or external tracking.

How it works:
  • Guest Request: Guest in Room 302 asks for extra towels via James
  • AI Processing: Gemini identifies this as a housekeeping request
  • Network Scan: System checks which housekeeping staff are currently connected to your Wi-Fi
  • Smart Routing: Ticket is sent to the nearest available staff member
  • Completion: No WhatsApp delays, no missed messages, no confusion

No GPS. No location tracking. No external services. Pure local network intelligence running on your property.
